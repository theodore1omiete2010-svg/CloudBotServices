1. Introduction

CloudBotServices ("CloudBotServices," "CBS," "we," "us," or "our") provides digital business automation and conversational tools. This Privacy Policy explains how CBS may collect, use, disclose, retain, and protect personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or use CBS services.

This policy is intended to describe our current practices. Because CBS is a developing service, features, integrations, processing activities, and service providers may change as the platform evolves. We will update this policy when those changes materially affect our privacy practices and where notice is required.

2. Scope

This Privacy Policy applies to information processed through CBS websites, accounts, dashboards, bots, automation features, and related services where this policy is presented.

If a CBS customer uses the Service to process information about its own customers, that customer is responsible for determining whether it has the necessary notices, permissions, legal bases, and other rights required to provide or process that information through CBS.

3. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use CBS and which features are enabled, we may process the following categories of information:

  • Account information: Name, email address, phone number, login/account details, and information needed to administer your CBS account.
  • Business information: Business name, business contact details, offerings, FAQs, bot configuration, automation rules, branding preferences, and other information you choose to place in the Service.
  • Usage and technical information: Information about features used, conversations processed, automation activity, timestamps, IP address, device or browser information, diagnostic information, and security or service logs where collected by our systems.
  • Conversation and customer data: Messages and other information exchanged between a CBS customer's business and its customers through supported integrations, including information contained in messages, orders, support requests, and automation interactions.
  • Billing information: Subscription, plan, billing status, transaction references, and related account information. Payment-card details may be collected and processed by payment providers such as Stripe rather than stored by CBS, depending on the payment flow used.
  • Support and communications: Information you provide when contacting CBS, including support requests and related correspondence.
  • Integration data: Information made available to CBS through third-party messaging or other integrations that you connect to the Service, subject to the permissions and configuration of those integrations.

4. How We Use Information

We may use information for purposes including:

  • Providing, operating, maintaining, and improving CBS.
  • Creating and administering accounts and subscriptions.
  • Processing billing and payment-related activities.
  • Running configured bots, workflows, automation rules, and conversation features.
  • Providing AI-assisted features where enabled.
  • Providing customer support and responding to inquiries.
  • Monitoring usage, reliability, performance, security, and abuse.
  • Communicating service, account, security, and administrative information.
  • Understanding service usage and improving features where applicable and lawfully permitted.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations and protecting the rights, safety, and security of CBS, our customers, users, and others.

5. AI and Automated Processing

CBS may provide a combination of rule-based automation and AI-assisted features. Where AI features are enabled, information submitted through the relevant feature may be processed to generate, assist with, or evaluate automated responses and other requested outputs.

The specific AI technologies and service providers used by CBS may change as the platform develops. This policy does not state that any particular AI provider is currently being used unless that provider is actually part of the applicable CBS feature.

Unless CBS expressly states otherwise in an applicable product or policy, CBS does not intend to use customer conversation content solely to train general-purpose AI models. Limited technical, safety, quality, or service information may be used as necessary to operate and improve CBS, subject to applicable law and the actual implementation.

Customers are responsible for configuring automation appropriately and ensuring that information submitted to AI or automation features may lawfully be processed for the intended purpose.

6. How We Share Information

We do not sell personal information or customer conversation data. We may disclose or make information available when necessary to provide CBS, comply with law, protect our services, or complete a transaction or business change.

  • Service providers: Providers that support hosting, infrastructure, security, communications, customer support, analytics, AI functionality, or other operational needs, where applicable.
  • Stripe and payment providers: Payment information and transaction-related information may be processed by Stripe or another payment provider used for the applicable transaction. Their own privacy terms govern their processing.
  • Messaging and integration providers: When a customer connects a platform such as WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, or Facebook Messenger, information may be exchanged with that platform as required to provide the integration. The third party's own terms and privacy policy also apply.
  • Legal and safety purposes: Where disclosure is required by law, legal process, or reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, security, or prevent fraud or abuse.
  • Business transactions: In connection with a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, sale of assets, or similar transaction, subject to applicable law.

7. Business Customer and End-Customer Data

CBS is a business automation platform. A CBS customer may use the Service to process information belonging to its own customers, such as names, contact details, conversation content, order information, or other information exchanged through a bot.

The CBS customer is responsible for determining what information it sends to CBS, configuring its automations appropriately, and providing any notices or obtaining any permissions required by applicable law. CBS processes such information to provide the services requested by the customer and according to the customer's configuration.

Nothing in this section transfers ownership of a customer's data to CBS. Contractual rights and responsibilities concerning customer content are also addressed in the Terms of Service.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, loss, or destruction. The specific controls may vary depending on the system, feature, and service provider involved.

We do not claim that any internet-connected system can be completely secure. Customers are also responsible for protecting account credentials, access tokens, connected integrations, and other credentials under their control.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the services, maintain accounts, meet operational and security requirements, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, comply with legal obligations, and satisfy other legitimate business needs.

When an account is deleted, CBS will take reasonable steps to delete or de-identify information that is no longer required for a legitimate or legally required purpose. Information may remain temporarily in backups, security records, billing records, or third-party systems according to applicable retention and deletion processes.

Specific retention periods may vary by data type, feature, account status, legal requirements, and the systems involved.

10. Cookies and Similar Technologies

CBS may use cookies, local storage, or similar technologies to remember preferences, support authentication and security, maintain functionality, and understand service usage where applicable.

The current public legal pages may use browser storage for preferences such as the selected theme. Additional cookies or analytics technologies may be introduced as CBS's website and application develop.

You can control certain browser storage and cookie behavior through your browser or device settings. Disabling essential technologies may affect functionality.

11. International Data Processing

CBS operates from Nigeria and intends to serve customers internationally. Information may therefore be processed in Nigeria or in other countries where CBS or its service providers operate.

Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, we intend to use appropriate safeguards and lawful mechanisms for those transfers. The exact processing locations and safeguards may depend on the service providers and features used.

12. Privacy Rights and Choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, which may include rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, object to certain processing, obtain a portable copy, withdraw consent where consent is the applicable basis, or opt out of certain communications.

These rights are not absolute and may be subject to legal exceptions, identity verification, security requirements, or other lawful limitations.

13. Privacy Requests

To make a privacy request, contact us using the email address below and describe the request clearly. We may need to verify your identity or your authority to make the request before responding.

We aim to respond within the period required by applicable law. If your request concerns information processed on behalf of a CBS business customer, we may direct you to that business customer or assist that customer as appropriate.

14. Children's Privacy

CBS is designed primarily as a business-to-business service and is not intended to be a service directed to children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children for their own independent use of CBS.

Because CBS customers may receive messages from members of the public through their own bots, a child may potentially communicate with a CBS-powered business. The relevant business customer is responsible for handling those interactions in accordance with applicable law.

15. Third-Party Services and Links

CBS may integrate with or link to third-party services. Those services may have their own privacy policies, terms, security practices, and data retention rules. CBS does not control the privacy practices of independent third parties.

Third-party integrations may change as CBS adds or removes supported platforms. We will describe applicable categories of third-party processing in this policy rather than treating every possible future integration as currently active.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as CBS's services, technology, legal obligations, or data practices change. We will update the "Last updated" date when changes are made.

Where appropriate and required by applicable law, we may provide additional notice of material changes through the Service, by email, or by another reasonable method.
