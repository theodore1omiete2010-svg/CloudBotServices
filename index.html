FAQ

Common questions

Which channels can I connect?

CloudBotServices works with WhatsApp, Facebook, Messenger, Instagram, Telegram, Bubble-Webchat, and other channels your customers already use.

Can I use my existing phone number?

Yes — there's no need to switch to a new number. Connect the number your customers already message you on.

What payment providers are supported?

CloudBotServices integrates with common payment providers to generate and verify payment links, so a customer can pay without ever leaving the conversation.

Can I take over conversations manually?

Any conversation can be handed to a real person at any time, and handed back to the bot once you're done — you're always in control.

What happens after my trial ends?

You'll be prompted to choose a paid plan. Nothing is charged automatically — your account simply pauses until you pick one.

Do I need any technical skills to set this up?

No. Setup is built for non-technical business owners — most businesses are up and running within minutes.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — every plan is month-to-month, with no long-term contract required.
